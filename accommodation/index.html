Dog-Friendly Accommodation in Dumfries and Galloway

Finding truly dog-friendly accommodation — where your dog is genuinely welcome rather than just tolerated — can be tricky. We've done the legwork for you. Every property listed here actively welcomes dogs, with direct loch or beach access, enclosed gardens, or dog-washing facilities.

Dumfries and Galloway offers everything from cosy self-catering cottages tucked into Galloway Forest to waterside lodges on Loch Ken and seaside B&Bs along the Solway. Whether you're planning a short break or a longer holiday, there's a perfect dog-friendly base here for you.

💡 Pro tip: Always confirm the maximum number of dogs allowed, any size restrictions, and whether there's a dog fee before booking. Our listings include these details where known.
