Dog-Friendly Accommodation in Dumfries and Galloway

Finding truly dog-friendly accommodation — where your dog is genuinely welcome rather than just tolerated — can be tricky. We've done the legwork for you. Every property listed here actively welcomes dogs, with direct loch or beach access, enclosed gardens, or dog-washing facilities.
