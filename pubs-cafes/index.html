Dog-Friendly Pubs & Cafes in Dumfries and Galloway

After a long walk through Galloway Forest or a blustery morning on the Solway coast, there's nothing better than settling into a warm pub with your dog at your feet. Dumfries and Galloway has a brilliant range of dog-friendly pubs, inns and cafes — many with wood-burning stoves, real ales, and a proper Scottish welcome.

All the venues listed here actively welcome dogs inside. Many provide water bowls, dog treats, and dedicated dog-friendly seating areas. Always check directly with the venue before visiting, as policies can change.
