Dog Friendly Pubs & Cafes in Dumfries and Galloway

Looking for dog friendly pubs near you in Dumfries and Galloway? After a long walk through Galloway Forest or a blustery morning on the Solway coast, there's nothing better than settling into a warm pub with your dog at your feet. Every venue listed here actively welcomes dogs inside — not just in a beer garden.

From a dog friendly cafe stop mid-walk to a full pub meal with your four-legged companion, Dumfries and Galloway has a brilliant range of options — inns, bars, restaurants and cafes — many with wood-burning stoves, real ales, water bowls, dog treats, and a proper Scottish welcome. Always check directly with the venue before visiting, as policies can change.
