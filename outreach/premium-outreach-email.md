## Personalisation Notes by Category

### Accommodation
Reference the setting (coastal / lakeside / rural / forest) and mention that dog-owning guests tend to book longer stays and return annually. Lead with Silver or Gold tier.

### Attractions
Note that dogs on leads are welcome and that many visitors plan their trip around dog-friendly stops. NTS / Historic Environment Scotland properties may need an org-level contact. Lead with Bronze or Silver.

### Pubs and Cafes
Mention that dog owners often search specifically for dog-friendly pubs before visiting a new area. If already listed (e.g. Murray Arms), frame as an upgrade conversation. Lead with Bronze or Silver.

### Services (Vets, Pet Shops)
Keep it practical: dog owners in an unfamiliar area always want to know where the nearest vet or pet shop is. A listing here is a genuine service to visitors. Lead with Bronze.
