The Ardwell Inn sits in the quiet village of Ardwell on the Rhins of Galloway, the long peninsula that stretches southwest from Stranraer towards the Mull of Galloway. It is the kind of traditional Scottish inn that is increasingly rare -- unpretentious, welcoming, and genuinely pleased to see dogs rather than merely tolerating them.

**The Rhins of Galloway**

The Rhins is a remarkable piece of Scotland, narrowing to a thin ridge of land with the Irish Sea and Luce Bay on either side. The walking here is wild and dramatic, with coastal paths, farm tracks, and quiet lanes offering routes for all abilities. Ardwell Bay itself is a beautiful sandy beach just a short walk from the inn.

**Dog Policy**

Dogs are welcome in all B&B rooms at no extra charge, which makes the Ardwell Inn exceptional value for dog-owning visitors to this part of Galloway. Dogs are very welcome in the bar and beer garden, where water bowls and dog treats are provided as a matter of course.

**Nearby Attractions**

Logans Botanic Garden, the Mull of Galloway Lighthouse (Scotland's most southerly point), and the pretty harbour town of Drummore are all within easy reach by car. The Rhins coastline offers some of the most undiscovered walking in southwest Scotland.
