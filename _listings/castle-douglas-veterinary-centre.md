Castle Douglas Veterinary Centre is the go-to practice for dog owners in the Stewartry area -- the central belt of Dumfries and Galloway that takes in Castle Douglas, Dalbeattie, Kirkcudbright, and the surrounding countryside. For anyone based in this part of the region, it provides accessible local veterinary care without the need to travel to Dumfries for routine appointments.

**Serving the Stewartry**

The Stewartry is one of the most beautiful and dog-friendly parts of Dumfries and Galloway, with Loch Ken, the Galloway Hills, and the Solway coastline all within easy reach. It is also a genuinely rural area, where having a reliable local vet matters. Castle Douglas itself is the main market town for the Stewartry, and the veterinary centre here serves as the practical hub for pet health across the district.

**Routine Preventative Care**

Annual vaccinations, flea and tick prevention, health checks, and worming treatments are the bread and butter of any good vet relationship. Dogs exploring the Galloway countryside face particular parasite risks -- ticks are common in the area, especially on moorland and in woodland. A local vet who knows the regional disease picture is a genuine asset. Ask about a suitable tick prevention programme when you register.

**Surgery and In-Practice Diagnostics**

For conditions requiring more than a routine consultation, Castle Douglas Veterinary Centre offers surgical and diagnostic services on-site, reducing the need to travel to larger facilities for many procedures. This is particularly valuable for older dogs or those with ongoing health conditions who may find long journeys stressful.

**Registering as a New Client**

If you are moving to the Castle Douglas area or spending an extended period in the Stewartry, registering your dog with the local practice before you need an urgent appointment is always the sensible approach. It takes only a few minutes and ensures you are in the system when it matters.
