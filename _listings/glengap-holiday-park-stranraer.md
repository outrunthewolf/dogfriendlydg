Glengap Holiday Park occupies a sheltered valley near Stranraer, offering a peaceful base for touring the Rhins of Galloway peninsula and the wider southwest Galloway coast. The park is family-run with the kind of unpretentious, welcoming atmosphere that makes it popular with returning visitors year after year.

**The Site**

The park offers a mix of static caravan hire and touring pitches for motorhomes, caravans, and tents. The valley setting provides natural shelter and a sense of seclusion, while remaining within easy driving distance of Stranraer, Portpatrick, and the Rhins coastal paths. An on-site walking area allows dogs to be exercised without leaving the site.

**Dog Policy**

Dogs are welcome on all pitches and in static caravans at a charge of just £2 per dog per night. There is no stated limit on numbers with prior notice, which makes Glengap a practical choice for households with multiple dogs. Dogs must be kept on leads on site and must not be left unattended in static caravans.

**Exploring from Glengap**

Portpatrick village and its coastal path are within easy reach, as is the wild walking country of the Rhins peninsula. Loch Ryan, with its ferry connections and wildlife interest, is just north of Stranraer, and the Mull of Galloway -- Scotland's most southerly point -- makes a fine day trip.
