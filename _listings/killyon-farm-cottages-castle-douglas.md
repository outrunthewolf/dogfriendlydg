Killyon Farm Cottages sit in the peaceful farmland south of Castle Douglas, offering a genuine rural retreat for dog owners who want space, quiet, and easy access to some of Galloway's finest walking country. The cottages are well-equipped for self-catering stays and each has access to private outdoor space where dogs can relax after a day on the trail.

**The Setting**

Set on a working farm near the village of Gelston, the cottages are surrounded by open countryside with field and hedgerow walks straight from the door. Castle Douglas itself is just a short drive away, offering excellent independent food shops, butchers, and cafes that make self-catering a real pleasure in this part of Scotland.

**Dog Policy**

Up to two dogs are welcome per cottage at a charge of £20 per dog per stay. Dogs must be kept on leads around the farm and its livestock, which is entirely standard for a working farm environment. There is a private enclosed garden area where dogs can be let off lead safely.

**Nearby Walks**

Loch Ken is within easy reach, with waymarked trails along the loch shore and through the Ken-Dee Marshes RSPB reserve. The Galloway Hills offer more demanding walks for adventurous owners, while the quiet lanes and field paths around Gelston are ideal for gentle morning strolls.
