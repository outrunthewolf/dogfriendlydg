The Black Bull Inn has stood at the heart of Moffat since the 16th century, and it has earned a remarkable distinction: it was voted Scotland's most dog-friendly establishment in the Daily Mirror and Daily Record's Team Dogs nationwide vote, placing fourteenth in the whole of the United Kingdom. That is not a title you earn by tolerating dogs — it is one you earn by genuinely welcoming them, and the Black Bull's reputation among dog owners across Scotland is thoroughly deserved.

## The Bar and Restaurant

The Black Bull's bar is everything you want after a day in the hills: low-beamed ceilings, stone walls, open fires that take the edge off a cold Galloway day, and staff who treat dogs as a natural part of the welcome rather than an afterthought. Water bowls appear without prompting. The kitchen makes good use of the excellent local produce from the surrounding hills and farms: Annandale beef and lamb, game from the Moffat Hills, and hearty portions that match the appetite built up on a long day at Grey Mare's Tail.

## Dog-Friendly Accommodation

Overnight guests with dogs receive the full treatment: a pooch welcome pack waiting in the room, complete with treats, food, a water bowl, and a soft dog bed. It is a detail that reflects how seriously the Black Bull takes its commitment to dog owners — this is a place where having a dog genuinely improves your stay rather than complicating it.

## Walking from the Door

The Black Bull is the natural base for two outstanding dog walks: **Grey Mare's Tail**, around 10 miles northeast on the A708, is one of the finest waterfall walks in southern Scotland, with a 60-metre drop and views over Loch Skeen at the top. Closer in, the **Moffat Hills** offer open moorland walking directly from the edge of town, and the **Annandale Way** long-distance route begins in Moffat, with the first few riverside miles well-suited to most dogs.

## Practical Information

- **Getting there:** Moffat is just off the A74(M) at junction 15 northbound and junction 17 southbound — a natural stop on the main Scotland–England corridor.
- **Parking:** Town centre parking is available on the High Street, a short walk from Churchgate.
- **Opening hours:** Check the website or call ahead — hours vary seasonally.
- **Booking:** Recommended at weekends and during the summer walking season, especially if bringing dogs.
