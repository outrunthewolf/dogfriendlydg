The Maxwell Arms Hotel stands on Maxwell Street in Dalbeattie town centre, making it one of the most convenient places in the area for walkers and dog owners arriving from the forest trails or the Solway coast. It is a pub and hotel combined — a traditional format that suits Dumfries and Galloway well, where the distances between towns make an overnight stay often more practical than the drive home.

## The Bar

The bar at the Maxwell Arms runs with the relaxed, inclusive atmosphere of a proper Scottish town pub. Dogs are genuinely welcome here — not tolerated in a corner, but treated as part of what makes a good country bar work. The staff are used to guests arriving with muddy boots and enthusiastic dogs after a day on the forest tracks, and the welcome reflects that.

Food is served, and the menu covers the pub classics that hit the spot after a long walk. The bar has a good range of drinks and the easy-going pace that characterises the better pubs of the Solway region. A beer garden provides outdoor space for those who prefer fresh air with their pint.

## Dalbeattie as a Dog-Walking Base

Dalbeattie is an excellent staging point for several days of dog walking in Dumfries and Galloway. The town sits at the edge of Dalbeattie Forest — a large managed woodland with well-signed paths accessible on foot from the town — and is within easy driving distance of the Colvend coast, Rockcliffe, Kippford, and the long beaches of the Solway Firth.

**Dalbeattie Forest** — extensive trail network starting close to the town. Varied terrain from easy woodland paths to more demanding hill routes. Dogs welcome off lead on most tracks.

**Rockcliffe and Kippford** — around 8 miles south on the Urr estuary. Two of the most picturesque villages on the Solway coast, linked by a coastal path with excellent dog access.

**Screel Hill** — a modest but rewarding hill south of Dalbeattie with wide views over the Solway. A good half-day outing with dogs.

## Accommodation

The Maxwell Arms offers pet-friendly rooms — useful for anyone using Dalbeattie as a base to explore the wider area over several days. Mention dogs when booking to confirm room availability.
