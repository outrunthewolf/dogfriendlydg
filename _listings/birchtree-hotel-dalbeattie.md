The Birchtree Hotel occupies a prime spot on Maxwell Street in central Dalbeattie, and after a recent renovation it has become one of the more appealing places to stop in this part of the Solway Hinterland. The refurbishment has freshened the interior considerably without stripping out the character — it's a comfortable place to arrive in after a morning on the forest trails, and the kitchen takes its food seriously enough to make it worth planning a meal rather than just a pit stop.

## Dogs in the Bar and Restaurant

The Birchtree has a notably relaxed attitude to dogs — they are welcome not just in a designated corner of the bar but throughout the bar and into the restaurant. For dog owners who find themselves being turned away from more restrictive venues, this is refreshing. The staff are experienced with guests who arrive with dogs after outdoor activity, and the approach to animal visitors is straightforward and unstuffy.

Water bowls are available on request, and the accommodation is pet-friendly — worth mentioning when you book to ensure a suitable room is allocated.

## Food

The kitchen at the Birchtree produces food that reflects the better end of Scottish pub cooking: seasonal ingredients, regional produce, and a menu that changes to stay relevant rather than serving the same frozen-to-table standards found in more complacent establishments. Given Dalbeattie's position in a county that produces excellent beef, lamb, shellfish and game, there is plenty of good raw material to work with.

## Dalbeattie and the Solway

Dalbeattie sits in a landscape that rewards the dog owner who takes time to explore. The forest is immediate — the Dalbeattie Forest trail network begins effectively at the edge of town, offering routes of various lengths through Scots pine and mixed woodland with almost no road walking involved. Beyond the forest, the coast is a short drive south.

**Dalbeattie Forest trails** — waymarked routes start from several car parks around the town. The forest is open, dogs welcome, and the terrain is varied enough to keep any dog interested.

**The Colvend Coast** — take the A710 south past Palnackie and Rockcliffe for one of the finest stretches of dog-friendly coastal path in southern Scotland. Rockcliffe's beach and the path to Kippford are highlights.

**Castle Douglas** — around 8 miles west, a useful market town with further services for visitors with dogs.
