The Cally Hotel is one of the most impressive dog-friendly hotels in southwest Scotland -- a grand Georgian country house set within 500 acres of private wooded estate on the edge of Gatehouse of Fleet. The hotel combines genuine grandeur with a relaxed, welcoming atmosphere, and dogs in selected ground-floor rooms means your four-legged companion can share the experience.

**The Estate**

The private estate is the real draw for dog owners. Hundreds of acres of mature woodland, open parkland, and estate tracks provide outstanding walking directly from the hotel door, without the need to drive anywhere. The estate borders Fleet Forest and the wider Galloway landscape, so there is no shortage of further exploration for longer days.

**Dog Policy**

Dogs are welcome in selected ground-floor rooms at £15 per dog per night, with a maximum of two dogs per room. The hotel estate is open to guests with dogs throughout the day. Dogs are not permitted in the restaurant, indoor pool, or leisure areas, which is standard for a hotel of this type.

**Gatehouse of Fleet**

The attractive small town of Gatehouse of Fleet is just a short walk from the hotel gates, with the Mill on the Fleet visitor centre, local pubs, and independent shops providing easy entertainment on rest days.
