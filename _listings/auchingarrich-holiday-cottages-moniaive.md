Auchingarrich Holiday Cottages offer some of the most dog-friendly self-catering accommodation in Dumfries and Galloway -- a cluster of lovingly restored traditional stone farm cottages set in the Cairn Valley near the charming village of Moniaive. Dogs are not just tolerated here but genuinely celebrated, with no extra charge, private enclosed gardens, and a drying room for wet days on the hill.

**The Cairn Valley**

The Cairn Valley is one of the quieter and more beautiful corners of Galloway, a green valley cut by the Cairn Water running south towards the Nith. The surrounding hills are classic Southern Upland country -- open moorland, forestry, and rough pasture -- with a network of farm tracks, drove roads, and hill paths offering outstanding walking for all abilities.

**Dog Policy**

Dogs of all sizes are welcome at absolutely no extra charge, with a maximum of three dogs per cottage by prior arrangement. Each cottage has its own private enclosed garden for safe off-lead exercise. Dog bowls, towels, and treats are provided as standard, and a drying room is available for muddy kit and wet coats -- an essential in Galloway.

**Moniaive Village**

Moniaive is one of Galloway's most appealing small villages, with a thriving arts community, the excellent Green Tea House cafe, and an annual folk festival in summer. The village is dog-friendly and easily explored on foot, with riverside paths along the Cairn Water providing gentle evening walks from the cottages.
