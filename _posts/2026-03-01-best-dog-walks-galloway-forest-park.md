Galloway Forest Park covers over 300 square miles of southern Scotland, making it the largest forest park in the UK. For dog owners, it is an extraordinary playground -- a vast, largely unfenced landscape of forest tracks, open hillside, rushing burns, and dramatic lochs where dogs can run, swim, and explore to their hearts' content.

Here are the walks we recommend most highly.

## Glen Trool -- The Classic

If you only do one walk in Galloway Forest, make it Glen Trool. The 8-mile circular route from Glentrool village follows the Water of Trool through ancient oakwood to Bruce's Stone, a granite boulder marking Robert the Bruce's famous victory at the Battle of Glen Trool in 1307. The views from the stone over Loch Trool are among the finest in southern Scotland.

Dogs love the burns and the woodland, and there is genuine off-lead freedom on the upper hillside sections. Keep on the lead near any livestock -- sheep are grazed on parts of the hill, particularly in spring and summer.

**Parking:** Glentrool Visitor Car Park, NX 415 804. Post-walk food available at the House o' Hill Hotel at Bargrennan.

## Murray's Monument -- Short but Spectacular

For a shorter outing -- perfect when time is tight or you have a young or older dog -- the walk to Murray's Monument near Newton Stewart delivers spectacular views for relatively modest effort. The 2-mile round trip climbs steeply from the Talnotry car park on the A712 to a striking granite obelisk commemorating Dr John Murray, a shepherd's son who became a distinguished physician and professor.

The open hillside is excellent for dogs -- good burn access, open terrain, and typically few other walkers. The views over the Galloway Hills make it feel much more dramatic than the distance suggests.

**Parking:** Talnotry car park on the A712 New Galloway road.

## Raiders Road Forest Drive

The Raiders Road is a 10-mile forest drive through the heart of Galloway Forest that is also a brilliant walking and cycling route. Multiple stopping points along the route give access to the Water of Dee, forest trails, and open moorland. Dogs can swim in the river at several points. The drive connects to the Murray's Monument car park at one end, making it easy to combine both on the same day.

## Practical Tips for Walking in Galloway Forest

- **Midges:** From late May through August, midges can be relentless in sheltered spots. Bring repellent and keep moving.
- **Ticks:** The forest is tick country. Check your dog thoroughly after every walk -- a tick remover tool is worth keeping in your walking pack.
- **Weather:** Galloway weather changes fast. Even on a fine morning, pack a waterproof for both you and your dog.
- **Livestock:** Always put dogs on leads when you see sheep or cattle, regardless of the terrain.

Galloway Forest Park is dog walking at its best. The scale of it means you can walk here dozens of times and never repeat yourself -- and the quality of the landscape makes every trip worthwhile.
